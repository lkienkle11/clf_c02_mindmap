## Why the six advantages matter

Many exam questions describe a company's situation and ask **which advantage of cloud computing** it shows. Learn each advantage together with the "clue words" that point to it.

## The six advantages

**1. Trade fixed expense for variable expense**
You stop investing heavily in servers and data centers before you know how they will be used. You pay only when you consume resources, and only for how much you consume.
Clue words: *upfront cost*, *pay only for what you use*, *variable expense*.

**2. Benefit from massive economies of scale**
A provider combines the usage of a very large number of customers. That scale lets it run more efficiently and pass the savings on as lower pay-as-you-go prices than you could reach on your own.
Clue words: *aggregate usage of all customers*, *lower prices because of scale*.

**3. Stop guessing capacity**
If you size hardware before launch, you either pay for idle servers or run out of capacity. In the cloud you use as much or as little as you need and scale up or down within minutes.
Clue words: *unpredictable demand*, *idle capacity*, *over-provisioning*.

**4. Increase speed and agility**
New resources are a few clicks away, so the time to give developers what they need drops from weeks to minutes. Experiments become cheap and fast.
Clue words: *experiment*, *faster time to market*, *minutes instead of weeks*.

**5. Stop spending money running and maintaining data centers**
The provider does the heavy lifting of racking, stacking and powering servers. Your team focuses on projects that set your business apart.
Clue words: *focus on customers*, *undifferentiated heavy lifting*.

**6. Go global in minutes**
You can deploy an application in several AWS Regions around the world with a few clicks, giving users lower latency at low cost.
Clue words: *users in other countries*, *global reach*, *lower latency worldwide*.

## Common traps

| Scenario says | Best match | Often confused with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Lower prices because AWS has many customers" | Economies of scale | Variable expense |
| "No idea how many users the app will have" | Stop guessing capacity | Speed and agility |
| "Spin up a test environment in minutes" | Speed and agility | Go global |

## Key points

- Memorize all six advantages by name; the exam uses this exact wording.
- Variable expense is about **how you pay**; economies of scale is about **why prices are lower**.
- Stop guessing capacity is about **uncertain demand**; agility is about **speed of change**.
- Go global in minutes relies on AWS Regions around the world.
