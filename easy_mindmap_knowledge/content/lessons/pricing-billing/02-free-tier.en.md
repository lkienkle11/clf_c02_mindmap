## The Free Tier changed in 2025

AWS changed its Free Tier for **accounts created on or after July 15, 2025**. Older courses describe only the earlier "12 months free" model.

## Current model: credits and two account plans

When a new customer signs up, they choose an **account plan** and receive **AWS Free Tier credits** that pay for eligible usage. They can earn extra credits by completing activities.

- **Free account plan**: for experimenting and building proofs of concept **at no cost**. It lasts up to **six months or until the credits are used up**, whichever comes first. Some services that would quickly use up the credits are not available on this plan. You are **not charged** unless you upgrade to the paid plan. When the free plan ends, AWS suspends the account and keeps the data for a limited time; you upgrade to keep using it.
- **Paid account plan**: for production work with **access to all services**. Credits still apply first; after that, usage is billed at standard **pay-as-you-go** prices.

## Types of offers

- **Always Free**: an ongoing monthly allowance for some services (more than 30), on both plans. Usage above the allowance is paid from credits, or billed on the paid plan.
- **Short-term trials**: free trials of selected services that start when you activate the service. They apply to paid plan accounts.
- **12 months free** (legacy): accounts created **before July 15, 2025** used the older structure of Always Free offers, short-term trials and offers free for **12 months after sign-up**. Those 12-month periods have now ended, but exam questions written earlier may still mention them.

AWS changes free amounts and credit values over time, so check the Free Tier page rather than memorizing numbers.

## Avoiding surprise bills

- The **Billing and Cost Management console** shows your current and forecasted **Free Tier usage**, and AWS can send alerts when you approach a limit.
- Create an **AWS Budgets** cost budget with an email alert as soon as you open an account.
- Free allowances have **monthly limits**; idle resources left running can still cost money on a paid plan.

## Common traps

- The Free Tier is not "everything is free for a year": credits and the free plan are for **new** customers, and every offer has limits.
- On the **free plan** you are not billed, but the account stops working at the end unless you upgrade.
- Always Free offers do not expire, but they have monthly limits.

## Key points

- Since July 15, 2025: credits plus a free plan (up to six months) or a paid plan.
- Offer types: Always Free and short-term trials; 12-month offers belong to the older model.
- Track Free Tier usage in the Billing console and set a budget alert.
