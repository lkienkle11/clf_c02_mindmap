## Optional: go deeper

This lesson is optional. Use it when you want to see the billing tools in a real console, or to review before practice.

## Suggested watching

Use the video search button to find a recent tour of the Billing and Cost Management console that creates a budget alert, explores Cost Explorer, and builds an estimate in the AWS Pricing Calculator. Prefer videos from 2025 or later, because the Free Tier changed in July 2025.

## Suggested reading

- [AWS Free Tier](https://aws.amazon.com/free/)
- [What is AWS Billing and Cost Management?](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/billing-what-is.html)
- [Analyzing your costs and usage with AWS Cost Explorer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/ce-what-is.html)

## How to use it

Pick a small web application (two EC2 instances, a load balancer and a database) and estimate it in the Pricing Calculator twice: once On-Demand and once with a 1-year Savings Plan. Write down which line items change the most.
