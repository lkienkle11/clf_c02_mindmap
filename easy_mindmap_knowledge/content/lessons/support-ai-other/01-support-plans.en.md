## Four plans today

AWS currently lists four AWS Support plans. Every account has **Basic Support**; the other three are paid plans billed monthly, with no long-term contract.

- **Basic Support** (included, no extra cost): 24/7 customer service for **account and billing questions** and **service quota increases**, documentation, whitepapers and AWS re:Post, the AWS Health Dashboard, and the **core** AWS Trusted Advisor checks. Basic does **not** include technical support cases with engineers.
- **AWS Business Support+**: AWS calls it the minimum recommended plan for **production workloads**. It adds **24/7 phone, web and chat access to Cloud Support Engineers**, AI-powered contextual help, the **full set** of Trusted Advisor checks, the **AWS Support API** and the AWS Health API, help with common third-party software on EC2, and unlimited cases opened by any user with permission.
- **AWS Enterprise Support**: everything in Business Support+, plus a **designated Technical Account Manager (TAM)**, a faster response for production-critical cases, strategic business reviews, access to AWS Security Incident Response, Trusted Advisor Priority and AWS Countdown support for critical events such as launches.
- **AWS Unified Operations**: the highest level, with a designated TAM and **Domain Specialist Engineers**, application architecture guidance, infrastructure event management and critical workload reviews.

## Comparing the plans

| Need | Lowest plan that meets it |
| --- | --- |
| Account and billing help, documentation, core checks | Basic |
| 24/7 access to engineers, full Trusted Advisor checks, Support API | Business Support+ |
| A designated TAM and strategic reviews | Enterprise Support |
| Domain specialists for mission-critical applications | Unified Operations |

Response targets depend on the **severity** you choose when you open a case, from general guidance up to business-critical system down. AWS documents a response of less than 30 minutes for business-critical cases on Business Support+ and up to 15 minutes for production-critical cases on Enterprise Support.

## Older plan names

Many practice questions use the previous plans: **Developer**, **Business**, **Enterprise On-Ramp** and **Enterprise**. AWS discontinues Developer, Business and Enterprise On-Ramp on **January 1, 2027** (they stay available in AWS GovCloud (US)). Developer and Business customers can move to Business Support+, and Enterprise On-Ramp customers are being moved to Enterprise Support during 2026. When an older question asks for "the lowest-cost plan with the full set of Trusted Advisor checks", its answer was Business; today the matching plan is Business Support+.

## Common traps

- Basic gives no technical cases with engineers; account, billing and quota requests only.
- A **designated TAM** points to Enterprise Support or higher, never Business Support+.
- The **full set** of Trusted Advisor checks and the Support API start at Business Support+.

## Key points

- Basic is free for everyone; Business Support+ is the recommended start for production.
- Enterprise Support adds a designated TAM; Unified Operations adds domain specialists.
- Developer, Business and Enterprise On-Ramp end on January 1, 2027.
