## Why look beyond a support plan

A support plan gives you AWS engineers. Many questions never need one. AWS also offers free community help, official self-service articles, ways to hire outside experts and ways to buy support through a partner. Exam questions test whether you can match the situation to the right resource.

## AWS re:Post and the Knowledge Center

**AWS re:Post** is a community question-and-answer site where customers, partners and AWS staff answer each other. AWS introduced it in December 2021 to replace the older **AWS Forums** (exam questions may still say "AWS Developer Forums"). It is free and open to anyone with an AWS account.

The **AWS Knowledge Center** holds articles and videos that answer common technical questions. Since March 2023 they live inside re:Post, marked with an **AWS Official** badge. You do not need a support plan to read them.

**AWS re:Post Private** is a private version of re:Post for companies with Enterprise Support or Enterprise On-Ramp Support, with spaces only your staff can join. AWS has announced that it will end support for it on **June 30, 2027**, so expect it only in older material.

## AWS IQ

**AWS IQ** was a service that helped customers find outside AWS experts for project work, and it appears in older exam material. AWS lists it among services whose support has ended (May 2026). If a current question needs an outside expert, think **AWS Partner Network** or **AWS Professional Services** instead.

## AWS Partner Network and Partner-Led Support

The **AWS Partner Network (APN)** is a global community of companies that build solutions and services on AWS. Partners include **independent software vendors (ISVs)**, **data providers** and **consulting partners** that migrate, build or run workloads for customers. Partners register and manage their membership in **AWS Partner Central**, and they sell through AWS Marketplace.

Some partners also deliver support themselves. With **AWS Partner-Led Support**, a certified partner supports you using AWS diagnostic tools and knowledge from AWS Support. For billing and account issues under resold support, you contact the partner, not AWS.

**AWS Marketplace** is the catalog where you buy third-party software and services on your AWS bill; the pricing and billing topic covers it in detail.

## Documentation and guidance

- **AWS documentation, whitepapers and best-practice guides** are free for everyone.
- **AWS Prescriptive Guidance** collects guides for planning cloud adoption and modernization, and patterns with steps and code for common migration and modernization scenarios.
- **AWS Trusted Advisor** checks your own account; the pricing and billing topic owns its details.
- **AWS Support Center** is where you open and track cases. With Basic Support you can open account and billing cases and service quota requests, but not technical cases.

## Which resource?

| Clue in the question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "No-cost community to ask questions and read best-practice articles" | AWS re:Post |
| "Official article answering a common technical question" | AWS Knowledge Center |
| "Third-party consultants to maintain or support the environment" | AWS Partner Network |
| "Paid engagements with AWS experts to speed up adoption" | AWS Professional Services |
| "Buy third-party software on the AWS bill" | AWS Marketplace |
| "Report abuse from an AWS IP address" | AWS Trust and Safety team |

## Exam traps

- re:Post is free and needs no plan; **AWS Support** is the paid service and is the wrong answer for "free community".
- The Knowledge Center is **content** that lives on re:Post, not a separate paid service.
- Partners are separate companies; **AWS Professional Services** is AWS's own team.
- AWS IQ is retired; do not pick it for a current scenario.

## Key points

- re:Post replaced AWS Forums; it is free and also hosts the Knowledge Center.
- re:Post Private is for Enterprise-level plans and will end on June 30, 2027.
- AWS IQ has ended; use partners or Professional Services for outside expertise.
- Partners (APN) are independent companies; some deliver Partner-Led Support.
