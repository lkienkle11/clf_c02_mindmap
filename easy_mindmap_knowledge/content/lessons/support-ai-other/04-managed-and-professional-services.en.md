## Support features versus services

AWS Support answers questions and helps fix problems. Several other offerings sound similar, and exam questions ask you to tell them apart. A good test is **who does the work**: AWS Support advises you, **AWS Professional Services** helps you build or migrate, and **AWS Managed Services** runs your infrastructure for you.

## Technical Account Manager (TAM)

A **TAM** is a person from AWS who knows your environment and gives proactive, strategic guidance. AWS lists a **designated TAM** with **AWS Enterprise Support**, and AWS Unified Operations adds a TAM plus **Domain Specialist Engineers**. The older **Enterprise On-Ramp** plan also provided TAMs. **Business Support+, Developer and Basic** have none. In the Unified Operations team, the TAM leads planning and quarterly business reviews.

## Concierge and billing specialists

The **Enterprise Support Concierge** team answers **billing and account questions**; it is not a technical or architecture service. The current plan comparison lists a **white-glove billing concierge** with Enterprise Support, and a **designated Senior Billing and Account Specialist** with Unified Operations. Basic Support already answers ordinary billing and account questions for every customer.

## Infrastructure Event Management and AWS Countdown

**Infrastructure Event Management (IEM)** is short-term help for a big event, such as a launch, migration or traffic peak: AWS experts review your architecture, scaling plans and readiness before the event and support you during it. AWS announced in 2023 that **AWS Countdown** replaces IEM and is included with **Enterprise Support**. **AWS Countdown Premium** adds designated engineers as a paid add-on for Business Support+ and Enterprise Support; Unified Operations includes one recurring subscription. Enterprise On-Ramp included one Countdown engagement a year.

A question that wants "strategic planning before a business-critical release, plus event management and real-time support during it" points to **Enterprise Support**. Exam items on this topic have keyed **Business** as the plan that includes infrastructure event management and operational readiness guidance at no extra charge. That differs from current AWS documentation: Countdown is included with Enterprise Support, and Countdown Premium is a paid add-on for Business Support+ and Enterprise Support. So when an item offers Business and Enterprise for "operational readiness at no additional charge", the keyed answer is Business, while strategic planning plus event management plus real-time support for a business-critical release is keyed to Enterprise Support.

## AWS Professional Services

**AWS Professional Services** is AWS's own consulting team. It helps you design, build, migrate and manage workloads, often together with AWS Partners, in areas such as data and AI, migration, modernization and security. It is a **paid engagement** and not part of a support plan. A question about a "global team of AWS experts" to speed a migration in line with AWS best practices points to Professional Services, not AWS Support.

## AWS Managed Services (AMS)

**AWS Managed Services (AMS)** is **infrastructure operations as a service**: AWS operates your AWS environment day to day, covering monitoring, incident management, patching, backup and security. Your workloads stay in your own AWS accounts.

AWS announced that **AMS Advanced** takes no new customers after June 30, 2026 and ends on **June 30, 2027**. **AMS Accelerate** continues; it works inside your existing accounts and covers incident, patch and backup management plus security monitoring, while you handle change and access management. Unified Operations and partner-led managed services are other AWS-suggested alternatives.

## Which one?

| Need | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Billing and account questions from a dedicated team | Enterprise Support Concierge |
| Designated advisor who knows my workloads | TAM (Enterprise Support or higher) |
| Expert help for a launch or traffic peak | AWS Countdown (formerly IEM) |
| Paid AWS consultants to migrate or build | AWS Professional Services |
| AWS runs patching, monitoring and incidents | AWS Managed Services |
| Outside company with AWS skills | AWS Partner Network |

## Key points

- TAM: designated with Enterprise Support and Unified Operations; the old On-Ramp plan had TAMs too.
- Concierge handles billing and account questions, not technical design.
- AWS Countdown replaced Infrastructure Event Management and is included with Enterprise Support.
- Professional Services consults and builds; AMS operates; Support advises.
- AMS Advanced ends June 30, 2027; AMS Accelerate continues.
