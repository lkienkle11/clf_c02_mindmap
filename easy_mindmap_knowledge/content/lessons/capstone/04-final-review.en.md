## Pre-exam checklist

**One to two weeks before**

- Read the exam guide's in-scope service list and look up any name you do not recognize.
- Pass the Capstone practice at 80% or more more than once, with different question draws.
- Work through your most-missed questions in History until you can explain why each wrong option is wrong.

**The last few days**

- Clear the daily reviews so no topic shows "Needs review".
- Reread the Key points of each topic and the keyword tables.
- Read the instructions for your delivery option (test center or online proctored) so the day has no surprises.

**During the exam**

- Answer every question; there is no penalty for guessing.
- Watch for MOST, LEAST and NOT, and for the number of answers to choose.
- Do not try to spot unscored questions; give each one your full attention.

## Common traps

- Picking a service you know well instead of the one that matches the wording.
- Mixing up similar pairs: CloudTrail and CloudWatch, Budgets and Cost Explorer, SQS and SNS, WAF and Shield.

## Key points

- Use the chooser table to find the topic to reread for each hesitation.
- Consistent Capstone scores of 80% or more are a good readiness signal.
- On exam day: read the ask, eliminate, and answer every question.
