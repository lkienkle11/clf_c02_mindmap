## Who registers, and where

You book the exam yourself. Sign in to your AWS Training and Certification account at **aws.training**, where the accommodation request is also made. The exam is delivered by **Pearson VUE**, so the booking you make there decides where and how you sit it.

## Testing center or online proctored

Both options deliver the same certification exam: **65 questions in 90 minutes**, with multiple choice and multiple response questions. The difference is where you sit it:

- **A Pearson VUE testing center**: you travel to a test site and take the exam there.
- **An online proctored exam**: you take it from your own location while a proctor supervises the session remotely.

The rules for each option, such as what you may have with you and how check-in works, are set by the test provider. Read the provider's instructions for your chosen option well before the exam day, because this app does not list them.

## The ESL +30 MINUTES accommodation

AWS offers a **30-minute exam extension** to non-native English speakers who take the exam in English. With the extension the exam time is **120 minutes**. The app's extra-time option uses the same 120-minute limit when you practise, so you can rehearse the longer time.

The request follows one important rule: **request it once, before you register for an exam**. The accommodation then applies to all future registrations, with any test delivery provider, so you do not need to ask again for later exams.

To request it:

1. Sign in at **aws.training/Certification**.
2. Choose **Go to your Account**.
3. Choose **Request Exam Accommodations**, then **Request Accommodation**.
4. Select the accommodation type **ESL +30 MINUTES**, then choose **Create**.

## A short checklist

- Decide between a testing center and online proctoring before you book.
- If you are a non-native English speaker taking the exam in English, make the ESL +30 MINUTES request before you register.
- Read the test provider's instructions for your chosen option.
- Plan for 90 minutes, or 120 minutes if the extension applies to you.

## Key points

- Register through your AWS Training and Certification account; Pearson VUE delivers the exam at a testing center or as an online proctored exam.
- ESL +30 MINUTES adds 30 minutes, for 120 in total. Request it once, before you register.
- The request path is Go to your Account, Request Exam Accommodations, Request Accommodation, ESL +30 MINUTES, Create.
