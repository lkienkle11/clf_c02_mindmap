## Who the exam is for

The **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** exam checks overall knowledge of the AWS Cloud, independent of a job role. AWS describes the target candidate as someone with **up to 6 months of exposure** to AWS. The exam does **not** expect you to write code, design architectures, troubleshoot, implement solutions or run load tests. Questions ask you to **recognize** the right service, concept or responsibility.

## Format at a glance

| Item | What the official pages say |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | 65 in total: **50 scored** and **15 unscored** |
| Time | 90 minutes. Non-native English speakers taking the exam in English can request **30 extra minutes** (120 in total) with the accommodation **ESL +30 MINUTES**; see the registration lesson |
| Question types | Multiple choice and multiple response |
| Result | Pass or fail, with a **scaled score from 100 to 1,000** |
| Passing score | **700** |
| Delivery | A Pearson VUE testing center or an online proctored exam |

## The two question types

- **Multiple choice**: one correct answer and three incorrect ones (called **distractors**).
- **Multiple response**: **two or more** correct answers out of **five or more** options. The question tells you how many to choose, such as "(Choose two.)".

Distractors are written to look plausible to someone with incomplete knowledge, so every option usually names a real AWS service or a reasonable idea.

## How scoring works

- **Unanswered questions count as wrong, and there is no penalty for guessing.** Always choose an answer, even when you are unsure.
- The **15 unscored questions** are being tested for future exams. They are **not marked**, so treat every question as if it counts.
- The score is **scaled**, which lets AWS compare exam forms of slightly different difficulty. A score of 700 is therefore **not** the same as answering 70% correctly.
- The model is **compensatory**: you need to pass the exam as a whole, not each domain. Your score report may show how you did per section, but those figures are only a rough guide.

## Keeping up with changes

AWS updates the exam guide from time to time and publishes changes about **one month** before they reach the exam. The guide has a revisions page, and its current version lists the new support plans and service names, such as AWS Business Support+ and Amazon SageMaker AI. Check the in-scope service list before your exam.

## How this app compares

- Topic practice lets you choose 10 questions, up to all the questions of the topic.
- The checkpoint exams and the Capstone exam are **65 questions in 90 minutes**, or 120 minutes with the app's extra-time option. You cannot review earlier answers during the attempt.
- The app's pass mark is **80%**, which is stricter than the official passing score of 700 out of 1,000 on the scaled score.
- **Every question counts.** The real exam has 15 unscored questions that you cannot identify, so the app treats all 65 as scored.

## Key points

- 65 questions (50 scored, 15 unscored and unmarked), 90 minutes; 120 with the ESL +30 MINUTES accommodation, requested before you register.
- Multiple choice (one answer) or multiple response (two or more answers).
- Scaled score 100 to 1,000; pass at 700; no penalty for guessing.
