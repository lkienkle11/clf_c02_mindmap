## Optional: go deeper

This lesson is optional. Use it in your last weeks of study to check yourself against AWS's own material.

## Suggested watching

Use the video search button to find a recent exam-preparation walkthrough. Prefer videos that mention the current support plans (Business Support+, Enterprise Support, Unified Operations); older videos may teach plans and service names that have since changed.

The exam prep page describes AWS Skill Builder resources, including free **Official Practice Question Sets** of 20 exam-style questions and free **Exam Prep** courses; Official Practice Exams need a subscription.

## How to use it

Take an Official Practice Question Set after you pass the Capstone practice here. For every question you miss, find the topic in this app that covers it and reread that lesson's Key points.
