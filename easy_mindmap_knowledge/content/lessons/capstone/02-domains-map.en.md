## Domain weights

The exam guide splits the **scored** content into four domains. The weight tells you roughly what share of the scored questions comes from each domain.

| Domain | Weight | Topics in this app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Cloud Concepts | 24% | Cloud Concepts; Migration and CAF |
| 2. Security and Compliance | 30% | Shared Responsibility; IAM and Security |
| 3. Cloud Technology and Services | 34% | Global Infrastructure; Compute; Storage; Networking; Databases; Deploying and Operating; Support, AI and Other Services |
| 4. Billing, Pricing, and Support | 12% | Pricing and Billing; Support, AI and Other Services |

Domains 2 and 3 together are almost two thirds of the scored exam, so spend most of your revision time there.

## Domain 1: Cloud Concepts

Four task statements: the **benefits** of the AWS Cloud (global reach, high availability, elasticity, agility); **design principles** through the six **Well-Architected** pillars; **migration** benefits and strategies, including the **AWS CAF**; and **cloud economics** (fixed versus variable costs, licensing such as BYOL, rightsizing, automation, economies of scale).

## Domain 2: Security and Compliance

Four task statements: the **shared responsibility model** and how it shifts between services such as EC2, RDS and Lambda; **security, governance and compliance** (AWS Artifact, encryption, logging and auditing with CloudTrail and Config); **access management** (IAM, root user protection, least privilege, MFA, IAM Identity Center); and **security services and resources** (WAF, Shield, GuardDuty, Inspector, Trusted Advisor security checks, Marketplace security products).

## Domain 3: Cloud Technology and Services

The largest domain, with eight task statements: ways to **deploy and operate** (console, CLI, SDKs, infrastructure as code); the **global infrastructure**; **compute**, **database**, **network** and **storage** services; **AI/ML and analytics** services; and services from **other categories**, such as messaging, contact centers, email, end-user computing, developer tools and IoT.

## Domain 4: Billing, Pricing, and Support

Three task statements: **pricing models** (On-Demand, Reserved Instances, Spot, Savings Plans, data transfer and storage tiers); **billing and cost management** (Budgets, Cost Explorer, Pricing Calculator, Organizations consolidated billing, cost allocation tags); and **technical resources and support** (support plans, Trusted Advisor, AWS Health, partners, Marketplace, re:Post, Professional Services).

## How to use this map

- Look up your weakest domain in the History screen's "Topics needing attention" list, then reread the matching topics.
- Questions often **cross domains**: a storage question can turn on cost, and a database question on shared responsibility. The Capstone questions practice that mix.
- The exam guide lists in-scope services by category; any service named there can appear, even with only one line in the lessons.

## Key points

- Weights: Cloud Concepts 24%, Security and Compliance 30%, Technology and Services 34%, Billing, Pricing and Support 12%.
- Domain 3 is the largest and covers most of the core service topics.
- Support plans and AWS Health belong to Domain 4; AI/ML and analytics belong to Domain 3.
