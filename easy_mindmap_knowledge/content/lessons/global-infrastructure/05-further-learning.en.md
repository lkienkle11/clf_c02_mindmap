This lesson is optional. Use it when you want to see the global infrastructure on a map, or to review before practice.

## Suggested watching

Use the video search button to find recent explanations of AWS Regions, Availability Zones and edge locations. Look for videos that show how they relate to each other and that compare Local Zones, Wavelength and Outposts.

## How to use it

Open the global infrastructure map and find the Region closest to you. Then check the Regional Services List to see which services that Region offers.
